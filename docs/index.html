Stretchtext turns any page into stretchtext: select a phrase, and a sentence or two appears right where you were reading. Click it to collapse. Paid with your own Pollen — no subscription, no middleman.

Stretchtext is an old idea: text that expands where the reader asks for detail Ted Nelson described it in 1967, but writing every level by hand was too much work, so it never caught on. Generated text makes the extra levels cheap enough to produce on demand. Click the underlined phrase to see it work.

Install in 60 seconds

  1. Download stretchtext.zip and unzip it into a folder you will keep.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  4. Open Stretchtext options → Connect Pollinations, approve the device code.

Using it

Select a phrase and click ⤢ Stretch. Or Alt+Click a word. The addition appears inline, follows the page's own text flow, and disappears when you click it.

Your Pollen stays yours: the extension receives a scoped key with a budget and expiry that you approve, and you can revoke it any time in the Pollinations dashboard. Only the selected phrase and its paragraph are sent for generation.
